About

I am an M.S. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Seoul National University. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Jae W. Lee, Director of the AI Institute of Seoul National University, and am a member of the Architecture and Code Optimization (ARC) Lab. I also collaborate with Prof. Sehoon Kim, an Assistant Professor at KAIST AI, who co-advises my current research in ML systems.

I am interested in efficient computing through the co-design of algorithms, systems, and architectures. My current research focuses on improving the inference efficiency of diffusion language models and hybrid-attention models through ML algorithm-system co-design. In the long term, I aim to build reliable and efficient long-horizon AI and robotic agents.

Research
